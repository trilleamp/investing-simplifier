Core EDGAR Sources for Forensic Accounting 
1. Annual Reports (10-K)
•	Financial Statements (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
•	Footnotes (details on revenue recognition, stock-based comp, contingencies, impairments)
•	MD&A (Management Discussion & Analysis) — management’s narrative often reveals adjustments, risks, or unusual items
•	Risk Factors — can hint at litigation, regulatory exposure, or accounting policy changes
2. Quarterly Reports (10-Q)
•	Interim updates on earnings distortions (restructuring, impairments, SBC)
•	Footnotes for short-term adjustments
•	Useful for tracking timing mismatches between GAAP and cash flow
3. Current Reports (8-K)
•	One-time events: acquisitions, divestitures, litigation settlements, restructuring announcements
•	Non-recurring gains/losses that distort earnings
4. Proxy Statements (DEF 14A)
•	Executive compensation (stock options, RSUs, performance shares)
•	Dilution impact from equity awards
•	Related-party transactions
5. Registration Statements (S-1, S-3, S-4)
•	Detailed breakdown of capital structure, warrants, convertibles
•	Pro forma financials that highlight adjustments
6. Insider & Ownership Filings (Forms 3, 4, 5, 13D, 13G)
•	Track insider selling/buying around distortion-heavy quarters
•	Identify incentives for earnings management
7. Specialized Filings
•	Form 11-K: Employee benefit plans (stock comp impact)
•	Form 20-F: Foreign issuers (same forensic approach as 10-K)
•	Form SD: Conflict minerals, supply chain disclosures (can reveal hidden costs)
