Formulas & Definitions

NOPAT

Net Operating Profit After Tax - the true operating profit available to all capital providers
NOPAT = EBIT x (1 - Tax Rate)

Capital Invested

Total capital used to run the business, from both equity and debt holders
Capital = Equity + Interest-Bearing Debt

Cost of Equity (CAPM)

The return equity investors demand for the risk of owning the stock
Ke = Risk-Free Rate + Beta x Market Risk Premium

WACC

Weighted Average Cost of Capital - the blended cost of all capital
WACC = (Eq% x Ke) + (Debt% x Kd x (1 - Tax))

ROIC

Return on Invested Capital - what the business earns on every dollar of capital
ROIC = NOPAT / Capital Invested

EVA (Economic Value Added)

The dollar amount of value created (or destroyed) above the cost of capital
EVA = NOPAT - (Capital x WACC)

ROE

Return on Equity - profit generated per dollar of shareholder equity
ROE = Net Income / Shareholders' Equity

ROA

Return on Assets - how efficiently total assets generate profit
ROA = Net Income / Total Assets

ROIIC

Return on Incremental Invested Capital - how well NEW capital is being deployed
ROIIC = Change in NOPAT / Change in Capital Invested

ROIC-WACC Spread

The value creation signal - positive means creating value, negative means destroying it
Spread = ROIC - WACC
Positive = Value Creator | Negative = Value Destroyer
